Privacy policy

Humans Back Then Uploader · Last updated: 29 October 2026

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for access to their YouTube account (videos, playlists, captions, comments) and read-only access to YouTube Analytics. It uses this access only for actions the channel owner starts: uploading the channel's own videos, editing their details, adding subtitles and thumbnails, and reading the channel's statistics and comments.

Where data is stored

The OAuth token and any data fetched from YouTube stay on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server of its own and does not upload this data anywhere else.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is also covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also ends access from that computer.
